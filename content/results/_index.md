Scores are kept with the PractiScore app, and each month's results link to the PractiScore web site. Stage designs are posted after each match. Results from 2015 to early 2017 were kept by hand and are shown here in full.

Thank you to all the match shooters for your help with setup, take-down and taping during the match. We couldn't do it without you! Welcome to all the new shooters: you help us grow the sport.
